# Revenue model and unit economics (Phase 6)

**Everything numeric in this document is a HYPOTHESIS or an illustrative calculation with stated inputs. No customer, revenue, traffic, subscriber or conversion figure exists for AIHubpedia, none is claimed, and nothing here is a forecast.** Prices are proposals awaiting owner approval; the public prices ($19 / $49 / $99 one-off) are unchanged and no EUR price has been published.

## How to read the maths
* Gross margin = (price − delivery cost) ÷ price. Customer lifetime (months) ≈ 1 ÷ monthly churn. LTV = price × gross margin ÷ monthly churn. CAC payback (months) = CAC ÷ (price − delivery cost).
* "Illustrative inputs" are placeholders chosen to show sensitivity. Replace them with measured values after pilots. A result that does not hold at the pessimistic end is not a plan.

## Unit economics (illustrative; recompute with measured data)
| Stream | Price / month | Delivery cost / month | Gross margin | Illustrative CAC | Churn 8% / 12% / 20% → LTV | LTV÷CAC (8% / 12% / 20%) | Payback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Featured placement (proposed EUR 199) | 199 | 40 | 79.9% | 300 | 1,988 / 1,325 / 795 | 6.6 / 4.4 / 2.6 | 1.9 months |
| Newsletter sponsorship (proposed EUR 499, **blocked**) | 499 | 60 | 88.0% | 600 | 5,488 / 3,658 / 2,195 | 9.1 / 6.1 / 3.7 | 1.4 months |
| Development project (one-off) | 20,000 per project | 60% of price | 40% | 1,500 | n/a (not recurring) | gross profit 8,000 → 5.3 × CAC; at a 8,000 project and 35% margin 2,800 → 1.9 × CAC | – |

Funnel arithmetic only (not a prediction): 100 inbound development inquiries at a 30% qualification rate and a 15% win rate yield 4.5 projects; at 20% and 10% they yield 2.0.

**What the table says:** the placement economics depend almost entirely on churn and on whether anyone buys at all; the first number to measure is demand, not margin. Services economics depend on win rate and delivery margin; one project outweighs many placements, which is why services are the first stream to pursue.

## Recommended order (hypothesis)
1. **Development services inquiries** (no audience needed; the CRM makes follow-up reliable).
2. **Partnership and implementation-agency relationships** (referrals in both directions).
3. **Featured placements**, only after a pilot with 2 or 3 vendors and verified profiles to show.
4. Newsletter sponsorship **last**, and only after a launched newsletter with measured clicks.

## Risks to profitability
Customer concentration (one or two sponsors), refunds and disputes, delivery capacity for services, currency and VAT handling, and the cost of editorial verification, which is the real cost of credibility. Overly aggressive monetisation can destroy the trust the product depends on.
