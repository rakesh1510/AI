# Futurepedia and AI-directory competitor analysis (Phase 6)

## Method and limits (read first)
* `futurepedia.io` **could not be loaded from the development sandbox** (no route; verified again with a direct request in this phase). No page was opened, measured or screenshotted.
* Everything below comes from **public web-search result summaries** retrieved on 18 November 2026. Several sources are third-party or competitor sites with an interest in the comparison; some are low-reliability, auto-generated pages. **Treat every competitor statement as an unverified claim.** No competitor revenue, traffic, subscriber or private metric is stated here as fact, and none was invented.
* Nothing was copied: no code, text, design, branding or content.

## Where AIHubpedia can be more useful, trustworthy and commercially attractive
1. **Non-purchasable verification.** If a competitor sells "verified" status, a label that only a named human can grant after a checklist is a credible alternative. It needs editors (owner action).
2. **Services-led revenue.** An audience-based sponsorship business needs an audience we cannot claim. TrustNova Tech development services can earn from relevant traffic without one: the service pages and guided inquiry are built for that.
3. **Transparent B2B.** Reporting definitions that say what is and is not measured (no impressions, no audience figure) can win vendors who distrust inflated numbers.
4. **Vendor-friendly evidence.** A vendor can see exactly which facts are documented, conflicting or stale on its own profile and supply a source, instead of paying to be "verified".

## What this analysis does not support
Any statement that AIHubpedia is larger, better, more trusted or more profitable than any competitor.
