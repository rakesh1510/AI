# Global growth and customer acquisition roadmap (Phase 6)

No outreach, posting, advertising spend or contact with any real business happens without explicit owner approval. Rankings, traffic and revenue are **outcomes to measure, not promises**. All targets below are hypotheses to test.

## Channel evaluation
| Channel | Fit | Cost | Evidence needed first | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organic search (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo) | High over time: evidence-gated, original pages | Editorial time | Verified profiles; decision on the 489 template pages (noindex recommended) | Slow; thin pages hurt |
| AI-search referrals (ChatGPT search, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot) | Medium: they cite specific, sourced, crawlable pages | Same content | Pages with direct answers, dates and sources; manual spot checks | No control; no promise of inclusion |
| LinkedIn | High for B2B services and partnerships | Time | Owner profile and approved messages | Low-value mass messaging; platform rules |
| AI vendor partnerships | High (referrals both ways) | Time | A verified-partner policy | Over-promising a relationship |
| Direct B2B sales (services) | High value per win | Time | Service pages (built), CRM (built), a reply routine | Unanswered inquiries |
| Startup and founder communities | Medium | Time | Genuinely useful posts, not ads | Perceived spam |
| Affiliate networks | Low-medium | Setup | A disclosure policy (exists) and a real programme to join | Trust erosion if recommendations bend |
| Educational content | High, compounding | Editorial time | Hands-on tests for workflows (none exist yet) | Thin content |
| Referral programmes | Medium | Rewards | Existing customers | Needs customers first |
| Newsletter | Potentially high | Provider, authentication, legal | Separate approval and launch | Not launched; sponsorship blocked until measured |

## 30 days: make the foundation real (owner actions first)
1. **Rotate historical credentials** (SECURITY doc). Not optional.
2. Apply the CRM migration to a **staging copy**, create the first owner, run the full test suite there.
3. Name the editors; verify the top 5 profiles; decide the thin-page policy.
4. Decide who answers inquiries and the service-level target.
5. Approve (or change) the service descriptions, the partner policy and the advertiser wording. **Measure:** inquiries recorded, time to first reply.

## 90 days: first pipeline
1. Launch to production only after the checklist (Phase 4/4.1/6 docs) and legal text for TrustNova Tech.
2. Publish the evidence-gated pages that pass their gates (task and comparison pages for verified tools).
3. With approval, **personally** contact a small number of AI vendors and agencies you already know (outreach drafts live in the CRM; nothing is sent from it).
4. Run 2 or 3 low-cost **placement pilots** with agreed measurable outputs.
5. **Experiments:** service-page inquiry rate by service; share of inquiries that qualify; partner applications per month. **Success:** a reply routine that never misses an inquiry; first qualified opportunities.

## 180 days: prove repeatability
Convert pilot results into pricing decisions (placement price, whether newsletter is worth launching); verify 20+ profiles; publish workflow guides backed by recorded hands-on tests; add 1 to 3 verified partners; review CRM data for the source that actually produces qualified leads and put effort there. **Measure:** won opportunities and value by source, renewal rate of pilots, cost of editorial time per verified profile.

## 12 months: decide what to scale
Choose between (a) services-led growth, (b) vendor-sponsorship growth, (c) both, using measured margin and churn (REVENUE doc formulas with real inputs). Consider a launched, consented newsletter only if audience data supports it. Revisit localisation with reviewed translations for markets that already send inquiries. Introduce per-editor accounts and MFA before any wider team access.

## Measurable experiments (all need approval where they involve real people)
| Experiment | Metric | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|
| Services page A: all ten equal vs featured top three | Inquiries per 100 visits to the services page | Keep the better after enough volume |
| Project form: with vs without budget field | Completion rate and qualified share | Keep if qualification does not fall |
| Partner page: wording of "who can apply" | Applications and fit rate | Keep higher fit |
| Placement pilot: labelled slot on category page | Attributed leads and advertiser renewal intent | Price only if renewal intent is real |

## Guardrails
Consent first; unsubscribes honoured permanently; no scraping of private personal information; no bulk or automated email; no fabricated testimonials, logos or numbers; sponsorship never changes editorial output.
