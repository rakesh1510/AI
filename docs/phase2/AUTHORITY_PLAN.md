# Authority and distribution plan

Principles: earn links and mentions with genuinely useful assets; never buy links, fake reviews or traffic; disclose commercial relationships; follow each platform's rules.

## Assets worth linking to (build these first)
1. **The sources-and-conflicts approach itself** — "Where AI vendors' own pricing pages disagree": a regularly updated dataset of conflicting vendor statements. Original, checkable, newsworthy.
2. **AI pricing and free-tier tracker** — dated snapshots of plan structures for reviewed tools, with change history once profiles are re-verified (change data only exists after at least two verification rounds).
3. **State-of-the-category reports** (e.g. meeting transcription plans) built from the guides' tables.
4. **Open data/tooling** — publish the taxonomy and classifier methodology (already transparent) and, if TrustNova Tech agrees, open-source the validation scripts; engineering credibility for AI practitioners.
5. **Embeddable comparison tables** with attribution.

## Channels (ethical, in rough order of payoff)
| Channel | First actions |
|---|---|
| Journalists and newsletters | Pitch the conflict dataset to AI/SaaS newsletters and trade press with a data summary; offer expert comment on pricing changes |
| Vendor relationships | Tell vendors when their profile is published and invite corrections with links (improves accuracy; some will link to the profile). Never sell profile changes |
| AI founders / builders | Offer a free, clearly-labelled review path (no pay-to-rank); founders cite accurate listings |
| Communities | Answer questions in relevant forums and communities where links are allowed; follow each community's self-promotion rules; no sock-puppets |
| Newsletter | Weekly "what changed in AI pricing and limits" from re-verification diffs; sign-up form is not yet built |
| Social | Share charts from original data; one account, honest branding |
| Guest contributions | Articles for relevant publications that stand on their own, with disclosure |
| Product directories / listings | Standard profile listings for AIHubpedia itself (e.g. software directories) where free and legitimate |
| Partnerships | Education/training providers, developer communities, accessibility of data via API/CSV for researchers |

## Guardrails
- No paid links, link exchanges at scale, PBNs, fake reviews, artificial traffic or engagement.
- Sponsored placements stay labelled; affiliate links stay `nofollow`; facts never change for payment.
- Keep a log of outreach and outcomes (referring domains gained, mentions) for the KPI sheet.

## TrustNova Tech credibility
Position as an engineering-led data publisher: transparent method, reproducible tests (CI), accuracy-first corrections. **Before any public company branding, resolve the legal-notice mismatch** (see RELEASE_RISKS.md): the current Impressum names an individual owner, not TrustNova Tech.

## 90-day plan
Days 1–30: instrument, verify Search Console/Bing, submit sitemaps, publish methodology, begin vendor outreach for the 20 reviewed profiles. Days 31–60: 3 more guides (after onboarding tools), first original data post, newsletter sign-up. Days 61–90: first pitch round, community participation, re-verification diffs published as the first change log.
