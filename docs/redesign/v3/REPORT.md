# AIHubpedia Premium 3D UI V3: final QA report

Branch `feat/aihubpedia-premium-3d-ui-v3` (on V2 `048a611`). Local verification only; nothing deployed, production untouched. Staging is behind Basic auth, so live behaviour is unverified.

## What V3 is
- **Design system** (`assets/css/premium3.css`): tokens, Space Grotesk + Inter (local, OFL), glass header with mega menu, premium dark footer, shared components (hero, bento, cards, forms, tables, badges, filters, state panels, doc layout). Per-page lighting tones (`page_tone()`), per-category and per-tool accents.
- **Pages redesigned:** all 52 inventoried public pages (list below), including contact, submit, services, partners, blog/article, about, comparisons, leaderboard, top, solutions, wizard, methodology, disclosure, legal pages, search, directory, tool, category, favourites, filters, use cases, changelog, newsletter archive, and 404/empty/success/invalid-link states.
- **Artwork:** 22 original Blender 5.3 (Cycles) studio renders, delivered as WebP with alpha plus a 560 px variant for phones (`assets/art/`, sources in `assets3d/`). Inline SVG remains only as a fallback if an image file were missing.

## Artwork assessment (honest)
The renders are genuinely path-traced (chrome, refractive glass, iridescent violet/blue/cyan coat, studio lighting, reflections, soft shadows). The hero knot, business robot, podium, shield, network, cubes and article covers are the strongest. They are **procedural and deliberately simple in form**: they do not reach the detail of artist-modelled, photoreal hero renders such as your references (no sculpted characters, no HDRI environment, limited material variety). Weaker scenes (compass, mail, workflow, book) read dark, so a tonal lift is applied at conversion. If you want references-level realism, the next step is commissioned or licensed models; specification: sculpted AI-agent character with subsurface/clearcoat materials, 4K HDRI studio lighting, 4000 px renders downscaled to WebP/AVIF, one scene per page type as listed in `assets3d/scenes/`.
Provenance and licensing: `assets3d/README.md` (all geometry/materials/lights generated by the scripts; no third-party assets; fonts under SIL OFL 1.1 with licence files in `assets/fonts/`).

Analytics/consent (`data-cta`, `cta_click`), affiliate/sponsored attributes and disclosures, `/redirect.php` counting, search, rankings, leaderboard formula, recommendation engine, CSRF/honeypot/time-trap/rate limits, newsletter and enquiry flows, CRM intake and admin, SEO metadata/canonical/JSON-LD, AI Universe hidden: covered by the suites above, all green. Pricing and business logic unchanged.

## Lighthouse (local lab; headless Chromium, simulated mobile throttling)
Two runs are kept: `lighthouse-raw-php-server.txt` (uncompressed `php -S`, pessimistic) and `lighthouse-gzip.txt` (gzip proxy emulating production `mod_deflate`; CSS 161 KB → 32 KB).
- **Production-like (gzip), mobile:** performance 99–100 on all 21 pages, accessibility 100, best-practices 96–100, SEO 100 (66 on `blog.php` and `search.php`, which are intentionally `noindex`), **LCP 0.9–2.3 s (all under 2.5 s)**, CLS 0–0.003.
- **Desktop:** performance 100, LCP 0.3–0.5 s, CLS 0.
- Raw `php -S` mobile: performance 88–98, LCP up to 3.6 s on image-heavy pages (no compression). Real figures depend on staging's compression and caching: verify there.
Best-practices 96 on some pages comes from third-party favicon requests blocked in the sandbox.

## Remaining limitations
- Artwork realism is below your references (see assessment). Procedural renders, not artist-modelled.
- Staging live behaviour, real mail delivery, CDN/compression and real logo loading are unverified (staging is auth-blocked; sandbox has no internet, so logos show monograms locally).
- Lighthouse numbers are lab figures; no real-user data exists.
- The `/admin-leads.php` check in `seo_test.php` needs a database (passes with the test database).
- Newsletter launch flag, booking/phone/WhatsApp, and payment/subscription providers remain unconfigured by design.
- `sitemap_lib.php`/`robots.txt` still reference the hidden AI Universe routes (they list pages only when verified data exists); decision pending.
- Deployment commands need the real staging host/user/path (placeholders in `DEPLOY_STAGING.md`).
