## 1. Website overview `[SOURCE + LOCAL RENDER]`

# CHATGPT HANDOVER (summary to paste first)
**Current design and functionality.** AIHubpedia is a PHP/MySQL, server-rendered AI-tool directory with a light/dark violet-blue "clean SaaS" interface: a hero with the H1 "Find the right AI tool, with the sources to back it up", a prominent search box, goal chips, three-path "How do you want to start?" cards (Discover / Compare / For business), category shortcuts, reviewed tool profiles with sources and "as of" dates, popular tools, guides, business bands, a long grid of all 509 tools, a newsletter form and a rich footer. Functions in code: search, categories, tool profiles, comparison, a recommendation wizard, favourites, votes/ratings/comments, AI Universe explorer, tool submission, advertiser enquiry, project inquiry, partner application, contact, double-opt-in newsletter, a click-counting redirect and an owner-only analytics dashboard. Existing 3D is limited to a floating, mouse-parallax card stack in the desktop hero plus tilting category cards.
**Existing revenue paths (none automated).** Paid listing and sponsorship packages ($19 priority review, $49 featured 7 days, $99 homepage spotlight 14 days; monthly and newsletter sponsorships unpriced), TrustNova Tech AI services enquiries, partner applications, and unused affiliate support (no default affiliate configured). **No online payment, no booking, no subscription product exists.** Clicks are tracked but are not revenue.
**Main conversion obstacles.** No call booking or phone; no checkout (paid plans finish by email/receipt); sponsorship packages without price or measured reach; business calls to action sit far down a very long page and are absent from the hero; the newsletter is described as not launched; mobile loses the hero visual and has many small tap targets; a visible "Ad placeholder" string on Contact.
**Recommended design direction.** Keep the trustworthy, source-first identity; add a "premium depth" look with restrained 3D (layered cards, glass panels, soft parallax on the hero) that respects reduced motion and performance; shorten the homepage and surface a business path in the first screen; give each tool card a clear primary action plus visible provenance (source/date/reviewed) badges; add dedicated, visible vendor and business conversion modules (claim/sponsor this tool, consultation with a booking option) and a simple purchase step for paid placements once a payment method is chosen. Do not promise audience, revenue or ranking.
**Technical constraints.** PHP page scripts with shared header/footer and card helpers; hand-written CSS with custom properties and a build step for minified assets; vanilla JS; forms use CSRF, honeypot and consent; click path `/redirect.php` must stay; sponsored labelling and editorial independence must stay; SEO markup, dark mode, reduced motion and accessibility must be preserved; Apache on Ubuntu; staging is protected by Basic auth and noindex.
**Missing information.** Live staging could not be seen (login rejected): confirm that this report matches it. Unknown: real traffic and conversion data, whether lead emails are delivered, which booking/payment tools the owner wants, price for monthly and newsletter sponsorships, brand assets (logo files, fonts) beyond the current system font stack, target markets and languages, and the owner's appetite for WebGL versus CSS-only depth.
